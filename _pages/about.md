Minghao Chen is a master's student in Informatics at Kyoto University, focusing on human mobility analysis, spatiotemporal data mining, and practical machine learning applications.

His research explores how movement patterns reveal social behavior and urban dynamics, and he develops data-driven tools to make academic work more transparent and reusable.
